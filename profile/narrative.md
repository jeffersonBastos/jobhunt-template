## Comp strategy

Keep a running log of compensation research and what you actually stated per
application, so you can spot patterns (e.g. consistently anchoring below
market rate) instead of re-deriving your reasoning from scratch each time.

- Research a target range per posting (funding stage, region, role level).
- Log what you actually asked for.
- After a few data points, look for a pattern — are you consistently
  under/over the researched range? That's worth correcting deliberately
  rather than repeating by default.

## Employment-status framing

If you're between roles, keep the honest framing of *why* documented here
once, so every application and interview answer stays consistent instead of
drifting. See `answers/bank.md` → "Why are you looking" for the reusable
spoken version.
