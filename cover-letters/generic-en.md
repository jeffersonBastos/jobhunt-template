Used for applications with no time to tailor, and as the starting point for
per-application tailors under `applications/<company-role>/cover-letter.md`.
See `research/cover-letter-best-practices.md` for the reasoning behind the
choices below.

The `[one line here...]` slot is the only part that has to change per
application.

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing about the [Role] position at [Company]. [one line here per application — something specific about their product or engineering work; this is the one slot that has to change every time]

[One short paragraph: your strongest, most verifiable achievement — ideally with a number attached and, if possible, public evidence someone could check.]

I like owning problems end to end: scoping them, building them, shipping them to production, and staying on call when they break. If that's the kind of engineer you need for this role, I'd welcome the conversation.

[Your Name]
