### Why are you looking / why leaving your current role
Recruiters hear this question constantly — short and unembarrassed beats a
long dance around it, whatever the real reason is (layoff, burnout, seeking
growth, relocation, etc.).

**Structure that works:**
1. What happened, plainly. One sentence.
2. Why it happened — frame around circumstances, not blame (whether that's
   the company's situation or your own forward-looking reason). One sentence.
3. What you're looking for now. Then stop.

[Your answer, PT and/or EN as needed]

**Follow-ups to have ready:** any details recruiters typically probe on (scope
of a layoff, timeline, what you've been doing since, references).

**Never:** badmouth a previous employer, over-explain, or dress up a
difficult exit as something it wasn't. Small industries remember
inconsistencies.

## Technical experience

Use this section for reusable answers to recurring technical-domain
questions specific to your target roles (e.g. "experience with high-volume
systems", "payment processing experience", "risk/ML experience"). For any
domain you *don't* have direct experience in, the reusable shape is:

1. State the gap honestly and briefly — don't stretch adjacent work into
   sounding like the real thing.
2. Bridge to the closest real experience you do have, and be explicit about
   what's actually analogous vs. different.
3. If relevant, name the general principles you'd apply even without direct
   experience in that specific domain.
