# Technical communication in English — running notes

Started during prep for an interview round. Log phrases, vocabulary, and
patterns worth keeping for any future English-language technical interview,
not just this one.

## Big-O notation, spoken

- O(1) → "O of one" or "constant time"
- O(n) → "O of n" or "linear time"
- O(n²) → "O of n squared" or "quadratic time"
- O(log n) → "O of log n" or "logarithmic time"
- O(n log n) → "O of n log n"

Natural interview phrasing: "this runs in constant time, O of one, since
it's a fixed number of arithmetic operations regardless of input."

## General approach

- Practice every algorithm/scenario answer **narrated out loud in English**,
  not just written — spoken fluency under pressure is a distinct skill from
  knowing the answer.
- Use scheduled English classes specifically for mock technical Q&A —
  ask the teacher to make you explain an architecture decision or walk
  through a solution live, not generic conversation practice.
- Record and listen back where possible — catches filler words and dead
  pauses that are invisible while speaking.
